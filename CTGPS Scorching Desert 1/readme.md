### CTGPS Scorching Desert 1
**CTGPS Scorching Desert 1** is a custom track created by GH0STP4WZ and Ironflash.
It is a remake of CTGPS Desert Road 1 by ToadetteHackFan.
The track was made for Toadette Hack Fan's Remake Casino 3 and served as GH0STP4WZ's first major project in custom track design.
